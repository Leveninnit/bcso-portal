Privacy Policy

Last updated: this page covers what the BCSO portal collects and how it's used. This is a FiveM roleplay community, not a real government agency — nothing here is a legal document in the real-world sense, but we still take the data you give us seriously.

What we collect

When you submit an application or an activity log through this portal, we collect exactly what's on the form: your Discord ID, your in-game character name, your badge number, your in-character rank, and whatever answers you type into the form's questions (why you want to join, your shift summary, custom questions a subdivision has added, etc.). Some subdivisions also ask for role-play details like FTO badge numbers or cadet Discord IDs on their activity logs.

We don't collect your real name, email address, IP address, payment information, or any government ID — none of that is ever asked for or stored by this portal.

How it's used

Every submission is posted to our Discord server so command staff can review it, and is stored in our database so it can be listed, approved, rejected, or deleted from the Command Access dashboard. If you're approved or rejected, that decision (and who made it) is recorded and may be shown on the Discord message and on the dashboard.

If you log into Command Access with Discord, we set a signed session cookie so you stay logged in — it only contains your Discord ID, username, and which subdivisions you have command access to. We don't use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising of any kind.

Who can see it

Applications and activity logs for a subdivision are visible to that subdivision's own command staff (and to anyone who can see the relevant Discord channel). Department-wide "Meet the Team" and subdivision leadership listings, Deputy of the Week/Month, and Patrol Photos are public — anything shown there was intentionally published by command staff.

How long we keep it

Submissions stay in the database until a command staff member with access to that subdivision deletes them. There's no automatic expiry — if you'd like something removed, contact a command staff member for the relevant subdivision on Discord.

Your choices

You can decline to submit an application or activity log at any time — nothing on this site requires an account, and browsing the portal (Applications, Activity Log info, Documents, Leaderboards, Meet the Team) never collects anything. If you want a submission you made removed, or want to know what's stored about a given Discord ID, ask command staff on Discord.

Questions

Reach out to BCSO command staff on our Discord server with any privacy questions about this portal.
